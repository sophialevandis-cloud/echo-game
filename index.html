ECHO
Level: 1/7
Attempts: 3
Level 1 — Go!

How to Play ECHO

Your goal: move the yellow player dot from the outer ring to the centre.

  • ↑ Up: move one ring outward.
  • ↓ Down: move one ring inward, toward the centre.
  • ← Left / → Right: move one position around the ring.
  • You must change direction each time: you cannot use the same direction twice in a row. There is also a short pause between moves.
  • Each successful move away from the centre creates an echo until that level's echo limit is reached. Level 1 has 1 echo, Level 2 has 2, and so on up to 7.
  • Purple diameter echoes travel back and forth along a straight line through the centre, bouncing at the ends.
  • Purple circular echoes travel around a ring. Echoes 1 and 2 are diameter echoes; echo 3 is circular, echo 4 is a diameter echo, echo 5 is circular, and so on.
  • New echoes cannot hit you during their first 0.4 seconds. After that, avoid touching them.
  • If an echo hits you, you lose an attempt and return to the starting point. You keep the same level, but all echoes are cleared. You have 3 attempts per level.
  • Reach the centre to complete the level. The next level is faster. Complete all 7 levels to win.

Tip: plan your next move before pressing an arrow. On a phone, tap the arrow buttons.
